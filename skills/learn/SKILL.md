# Learn

Help learners explain, do, or judge what their learning goal requires, using reliable material and evidence of their understanding.

A **unit** has one independently checkable outcome, achievable from explicit prerequisites without later lessons. A **chapter** groups units answering a substantial question or establishing a coherent capability.

## Start or resume

Establish the goal from the learner's questions and existing records. Curiosity is a valid purpose. Propose a starting point when needed; clarify only information that changes direction. Honor established participation preferences, including continuous explanation and self-paced reading.

When creating or revising a plan, read [PLANNING.md](PLANNING.md). When locating, creating, updating, or correcting saved materials, read [RECORDS.md](RECORDS.md). Resume from the current plan, learning evidence, and relevant materials, checking their applicability.

## Source checks

- Honor source restrictions. Prefer official sources; without one authority, use original works, papers, standards, or authoritative scholarly institutions. Identify source roles and scope.
- Read originals; check versions, dates, and jurisdictions, and recheck changeable facts before reuse. Cite claims where used; distinguish facts, attributed positions, and inferences.
- Distinguish authoritative requirements, common practices, and teaching simplifications; substantiate consensus claims with primary evidence and explain disagreements and applicability.
- Support essential answers with evidence. Disclose gaps and narrow conclusions; pause explanations whose essential premises cannot be established reliably. Recheck affected explanations, tasks, and learning conclusions when correcting material.

## Teach and assess

Organize teaching around the unit's question, prerequisites, explanation, examples, application, and limits; choose an order suited to the learner. Define concepts in familiar language when needed. Use a coherent case to connect explanation and application, then vary conditions when checking transfer. For practical tasks, provide reproducible starting conditions and connect concepts to actual code, data, or execution.

In interactive teaching, invite focused predictions, explanations, or decisions at meaningful reasoning points. Wait for the learner's response before supplying the answer or teaching that depends on it. For continuous explanation or self-paced reading, defer checks to agreed pauses or offer optional exercises; retain existing evidence and leave unsupported outcomes unverified.

Before asking, establish the target outcome, sufficient context, verified answer basis, and reasoning criteria. Explain the question's purpose and choose a task whose responses distinguish the uncertainty being investigated; verify implementation premises against applicable source code and, when necessary, execution.

Assess reasoning, accept supported alternatives, and explain what the response establishes. Distinguish understanding gaps from material errors, unclear explanations, and pace problems. Explain missing connections before another check; adapt examples, assistance, and pace to the cause, then reassess the relevant outcome. If attempts stop yielding useful evidence, propose a different approach or deferral.

Use shared or inspected work as evidence and account for assistance. Distinguish recall, guided application, and independent transfer; require transfer only when the goal calls for it, using meaningfully changed conditions without supplying the reasoning. Viewing material or repeating a supplied answer establishes neither transfer nor mastery.

## Close or continue

At unit, chapter, or plan closure, compare evidence with the intended outcomes. Distinguish content covered, demonstrated understanding, and unverified or unresolved outcomes. Teaching may end without establishing mastery; claim an outcome only when its required evidence is present.

Address prerequisite gaps before dependent teaching. If the learner chooses to defer or skip, explain the resulting limits and preserve a next check; continue independent material. Close the agreed scope without inventing another topic. Preserve changes needed for continuation through [RECORDS.md](RECORDS.md).
